## Finding

The US Treasury in 2026 maintains a "benchmark" approach, focusing on predictable issuance to maintain market depth.

### Issuance Mix
- **Bill Dominance**: Short-term bills remain elevated to manage TGA volatility and provide flexible funding.
- **Coupon Stability**: Gradual increases in coupon issuance to meet structural deficit needs without triggering "term premium" shocks.
- **Liquidity Management**: Active use of buybacks to manage maturity profile and improve liquidity in off-the-run segments.

## Evidence Chain
Fiscal Deficit → Increased Financing Need → T-Bill "buffer" + gradual Coupon increase → Manageable duration supply [WEB-2026-05-24].
