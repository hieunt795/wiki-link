## Finding

Inflation in 2026 is trending toward the 2% target but remains vulnerable to structural stickiness. The primary factors include:
1. **Housing/Shelter**: Moderating with a lag, still the largest component of core CPI.
2. **Services Ex-Housing**: Driven by labor market tightness; wage growth is slowing but remains above levels consistent with 2% inflation.
3. **Supply-Side Resilience**: Supply chains have largely normalized, but geopolitical fragmentation poses sporadic upward pressure on PPI components (energy/materials).

## Evidence Chain
Labor market tightness → Wage growth → Service sector inflation → Core CPI stickiness [WEB-2026-05-24].

## Limitations / Caveats
Projections are highly sensitive to labor participation rates and potential geopolitical shocks affecting global energy prices.
