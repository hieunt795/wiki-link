## Finding

The US term premium in 2026 is driven by structural factors related to the supply-demand balance of Treasuries rather than just monetary policy expectations.

### Drivers
1. **Supply Risk**: High and persistent fiscal deficits necessitate ongoing large-scale Treasury issuance, demanding a risk premium from investors.
2. **QT/Supply Absorption**: The transition from Fed as a "price-insensitive buyer" to a "seller/passive roll-off" increases private sector absorption risk.
3. **Correlation Risk**: The breakdown of the bond-equity diversification relationship (bonds as a hedge for equities) increases the risk premium demanded by investors.

## Evidence Chain
High Fiscal Deficits + QT → Increased Supply Risk → Higher Term Premium demand by private investors [WEB-2026-05-24].
