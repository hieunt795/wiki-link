## Finding

Fed QT in 2026 is transitioning from "easy" liquidity absorption (via ON RRP) to more sensitive "reserve drain" territory. As ON RRP balances decline, QT directly reduces bank reserves.

### Liquidity Impacts
- **Reserve Scarcity**: As ON RRP approaches zero, the "drain" falls squarely on bank reserves.
- **Intermediation Friction**: Banks are increasingly constrained by SLR/LCR, making it harder to absorb reserves and intermediate the Treasury market, increasing the importance of facilities like the SRF.

## Evidence Chain
ON RRP exhaustion → Reserve drain → Increased interbank funding cost/Repo volatility → SRF usage as "soft ceiling" [WEB-2026-05-24].
