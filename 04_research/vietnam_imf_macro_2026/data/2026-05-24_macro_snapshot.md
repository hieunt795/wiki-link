Sources: [WEB-2026-05-24 CEIC/SBV/IMF]

**CA Structure (2024):**
- Goods: large surplus — Vietnam exports electronics (Samsung ~20% GDP), textiles, footwear
- Services: deficit — tourism not fully recovered, freight payments
- Income: deficit — profit repatriation by FDI firms (Samsung, Intel, etc.)
- Transfers: surplus — remittances ~$16-18bn (~4% GDP)
- Net: +6.1% GDP [WEB-2026-05-24]

**FX Reserve Adequacy — IMF Assessment:**
```
Actual:     ~$80-83bn = 2.4 months imports
IMF target: 3-4 months imports (12-14 weeks) = adequate
Gap:        ~0.9-1.6 months short → "moderately below adequacy"
```
→ SBV sold $9.3bn in 2024 defending VND band → reserves depleted from ~$90bn (2023) to ~$83bn
→ Since early 2025: SBV refrained from spot intervention, adjusted center rate instead

**Exchange Rate Policy:**
- Managed float with ±5% band around SBV center rate
- Center rate adjusted periodically → crawling peg de facto
- 2024: depreciation pressure from USD strength → SBV defended then adjusted

**Monetary Survey Identity (M2 = NFA + NDA):**
```
M2 = NFA_MA + NDA
NDA = NCG + CPS + OIN

2024 dynamics:
  NFA ↓  (SBV sold $9.3bn FX → NFA giảm → contractionary impulse)
  CPS ↑  (credit growth 15% → expansionary impulse)
  NCG    (SBV không tài trợ fiscal trực tiếp → NCG roughly flat)
  Net M2: modest expansion despite NFA drag
```

**SBV Balance Sheet — MA Level (estimated):**
```
ASSETS                          LIABILITIES
NFA (FX reserves ~$83bn)        Reserve Money
NCG (small — no monetization)     Currency in circulation
CPS (direct CB loans minimal)     Bank deposits at SBV
Claims on banks (refinancing)   Govt deposits
                                Capital + OIN
```
→ NFA là dominant asset của SBV (>70% balance sheet ước tính)
→ NFA volatility = primary driver của reserve money in Vietnam

**Credit Channel:**
- 134% credit/GDP là RỦINGUY CAO cho EM — well above median (~90-100%)
- Real estate credit: significant portion → NPL risk nếu property market suy yếu
- Credit growth 19% (Jun 2025) driven bởi: low lending rates + RE recovery + policy push

**Key Macro Tensions:**

1. **NFA drain vs Credit boom**: SBV mất dự trữ 2024 nhưng credit vẫn tăng 15-19% → M2 được bù bởi NDA tăng (CPS) → expansionary monetary stance dù NFA giảm

2. **Fiscal expansion 2025**: deficit 3.3% GDP cần tài trợ ~$12-15bn → domestic bond market phải hấp thụ nhiều hơn → crowding out risk nếu banks đã overextended (credit/GDP 134%)

3. **CA surplus narrowing**: từ 6.1% (2024) xuống 4% (2025f) do tariff shock (US tariffs 20% avg) → export growth chậm lại → foreign savings của Việt Nam giảm

4. **Reserve adequacy**: 2.4 months << 3-4 months target → limited buffer nếu capital flow reversal hay sudden stop

## 6. QUASI-FISCAL — IMF GFS ADJUSTMENT

**Identified Quasi-Fiscal Operations (QFOs) chưa trong GFS thông thường:**

| QFO | Mechanism | Ước tính quy mô |
|-----|-----------|-----------------|
| VBSP below-market lending | Chênh lệch lãi suất × VND 398 trillion | ~0.2-0.3% GDP/năm |
| VDB policy lending | Off-budget contingent liabilities | Chưa rõ |
| SBV sterilization cost | Lãi T-bill phát hành × volume | ~0.1-0.2% GDP |
| SBV FX intervention loss | Sell high/buy low FX cycle | Variable |

→ **True fiscal stance** = GFS deficit + QFOs ≈ -2% to -2.5% GDP (2024) thay vì -1.5% reported
→ IMF GFS framework nhận diện vấn đề này nhưng Vietnam chưa công bố full quasi-fiscal reconciliation
