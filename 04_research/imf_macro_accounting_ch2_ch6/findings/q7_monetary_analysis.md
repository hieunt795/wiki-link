## Core Finding

Ch.5 frames monetary analysis as a two-step process:
1. **Accounting step**: construct the monetary survey (NFA + NDA = M0)
2. **Analytical step**: explain money demand, set NDA ceiling, monitor transmission

The NDA ceiling is the operational expression of monetary programming — it is the constraint that ensures the government's fiscal borrowing from the banking system does not exceed what is consistent with the external balance and inflation targets. [RAW-BOOK Ch.5, Financial Programming section]

Special issues in transition economies: dollarization (substitution of foreign for domestic currency), rapid financial deepening, unreliable velocity estimates — all undermine the stability of money demand functions needed for programming. [RAW-BOOK Ch.5, pp.146-154]
