### Seigniorage and Inflation Tax [RAW-BOOK Ch.3 Box 3.1, p.64]
Key claims from source:
- Seigniorage = revenue from money creation = ΔReserve Money (in real terms)
- Inflation tax = public's real losses from holding depreciating cash balances
- When real cash balances constant: seigniorage = inflation tax
- Inflation tax is INEFFICIENT: public's cost >> government's revenue
- Inverted-U relationship: as inflation rises, seigniorage rises then falls (Laffer curve for money)
- Base erosion: higher inflation → dollarization → shrinking tax base → need even higher inflation to maintain revenue

### Expenditure Arrears [RAW-BOOK Ch.3 Box 3.3, p.67]
Key claims from source:
- Arrears = government fails to meet expenditure commitments on time (especially interest)
- Creates wedge between accrual deficit and cash deficit
- Accrual deficit: full interest obligations (true resource cost)
- Cash deficit: actual interest payments made
- If cash < accrual → government is accumulating arrears (hidden deficit)
- Interest arrears may be capitalized, adding to debt stock
