## Core Finding

Ch.3 distinguishes three deficit concepts that answer different questions:
- **Conventional deficit**: overall fiscal position (what is the government borrowing?)
- **Operational deficit**: inflation-adjusted (what is the real resource cost?)
- **Primary deficit**: sustainability test (is debt/GDP rising or falling at current policies?)

Sustainability condition [RAW-BOOK Ch.3, p.66]: if r > g (real interest rate > real growth rate), a primary surplus is needed to stabilize debt/GDP. This is the fundamental fiscal solvency test.

Government sector boundary: GFS uses a combination of financing (mainly government) and market sales (< 50% of costs) criteria to determine what entities belong to general government vs public enterprises. [RAW-BOOK Ch.3, p.54-56]
