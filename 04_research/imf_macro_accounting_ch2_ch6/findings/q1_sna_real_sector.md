## Core Finding

Ch.2 establishes two layers:

**Layer 1 — Measurement:** The SNA provides three equivalent approaches to GDP. The 1993 SNA adds a full sequence of accounts (production, income, capital, financial) that links real sector to flow of funds.

**Layer 2 — Accounting constraints:** The two basic identities (CAB = GNDI − A and CAB = S − I) are ex-post accounting constraints — always true, not theories of behavior. They define the *space* within which adjustment policy must operate.

Key distinction [RAW-BOOK Ch.2, p.19]: these relationships "always hold, but they do not provide an explanation of any imbalances in the economy."
