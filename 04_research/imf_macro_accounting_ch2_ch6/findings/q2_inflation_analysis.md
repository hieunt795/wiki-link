## Core Finding

Ch.2 frames inflation analysis as requiring simultaneous tracking of:
1. The *source* (demand vs cost vs structural vs policy)
2. The *measure* (CPI for welfare; GDP deflator for output price; PPI for upstream)
3. The *propagation* (wage-price spiral, indexation, expectations)

Incomes policy (wage controls) is presented as necessary when fiscal and monetary restraint alone cannot break entrenched inflation-wage dynamics — a heterodox complement, not a substitute for orthodox stabilization. [RAW-BOOK Ch.2, Incomes Policy, p.26]
