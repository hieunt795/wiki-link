### External Debt Dynamics [RAW-BOOK Ch.4, pp.110-115]
Debt stock equation:
  D_t = D_{t-1} + B_t − A_t + valuation adjustments

Where:
- B_t = new disbursements
- A_t = amortization (principal repayments)
- Valuation adjustments: debt in foreign currencies changes in domestic currency value when FX moves; interest arrears capitalized

**Key debt sustainability indicators:**
- Debt/GDP: solvency (stock vs output capacity)
- Debt/Exports: solvency (stock vs FX earnings capacity)  
- Debt service/Exports: liquidity (annual payments vs FX inflows)
  - Debt service = interest + amortization
  - Rule of thumb: >25% is problematic

### Reserve Adequacy [RAW-BOOK Ch.4, pp.113-115]
Three metrics:
1. **Import cover**: Gross Reserves / Monthly Import Bill ≥ 3 months (rule of thumb)
2. **Short-term debt coverage** (Greenspan-Guidotti): Reserves ≥ Short-term debt
3. **Policy credibility**: fixed rate needs more reserves than float; credible policies can substitute for reserve level

Critical nuance [RAW-BOOK Ch.4, p.115]: reserve adequacy depends on exchange rate regime AND policy credibility, not just import coverage mechanically. Poland maintained a fixed rate in 1991 with moderate reserves because market confidence was high.
