# Q8: Flow of Funds — 4-Sector Consistency and Macroeconomic Interrelations

## Coverage State: INGESTED (full)

## Key Wiki Nodes

  - Four sectors: households, non-financial corporations, government, rest of world
  - Core identity: Σ(Net Lending/Borrowing) = 0 across all sectors
  - Each sector's NL/NB = S − I = financial account balance
  - Recording conventions: uses of funds (+ = increase in assets or decrease in liabilities); sources of funds (+ = decrease in assets or increase in liabilities)

  - How imbalances in one sectoral account transmit to others via flow of funds
  - Three crisis vectors: fiscal → monetary, external → banking, banking → fiscal

## Core Finding

Ch.6 is the integrating chapter: the Flow of Funds framework is the accounting structure that unifies Ch.2–5 into a single consistent framework. Each sectoral account (real sector from Ch.2, fiscal from Ch.3, BOP from Ch.4, monetary from Ch.5) feeds into one row/column of the Flow of Funds matrix.

**Consistency check**: if all four sector accounts are correctly constructed, the matrix rows (uses = sources) and columns (each sector's S−I = NL/NB) must balance. Any discrepancy (errors and omissions) signals data inconsistency. This is the operational use: the flow of funds matrix as a macroeconomic consistency framework. [RAW-BOOK Ch.6, pp.169-176]

**Sign conventions** [RAW-BOOK Ch.6 Box 6.3, p.174]:
- Increase in financial asset = use of funds (+)
- Decrease in financial asset = source of funds (−)
- Increase in liability = source of funds (+)
- Decrease in liability = use of funds (−)
For each sector: NL/NB = total sources − total uses = S − I

**Key relationship** (from Ch.6 Box 6.1):
  Private NL/NB + Government NL/NB + RoW NL/NB = 0
  (Private S − I) − (G − T) = (X − M + factor income + transfers)
  Private sector surplus = Fiscal deficit + Current account surplus
