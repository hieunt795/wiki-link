# Q6: Monetary Survey — NDA/NFA, Consolidation, Money Multiplier

## Core Finding

Ch.5 introduces the **consolidation methodology**: combining monetary authorities (CB) balance sheet with deposit money banks (DMBs) eliminates inter-bank claims and produces the monetary survey. The resulting identity NFA + NDA = Reserve Money is the foundation for monetary programming.

Critical insight: NFA changes have two sources — (1) genuine BOP transactions and (2) valuation adjustments from FX moves. Confusing them distorts the NDA ceiling calculation in IMF financial programming. [RAW-BOOK Ch.5, Box 5.8, p.143]
