## April 2026 FOMC Signal Shift

**Key change:** FOMC **removed easing bias** from statement language. [WEB-2026-05-24]

**Reason:** Inflation re-acceleration —
- PCE total: 2.8% (Feb) → 3.5% estimate (Mar) [WEB-2026-05-24]
- Core PCE: 3.0% (Feb) → 3.2% estimate (Mar)
- Driver: energy prices + tariff pass-through effects [WEB-2026-05-24]

**One dissent:** One member voted to cut 25bps, citing labor market downside risks.
**Unemployment:** 4.3%, stable. Job gains modest.
**Staff view:** Inflation expected to slow after mid-2026 as tariff effects fade → approach 2% by end-2027.

## Rate Path Implications (T_MODE_DEEP)

**Condition A — Baseline (tariff effects transitory, inflation peaks Q2 2026):**
FFR 3.5-3.75% held through summer → one 25bps cut Q4 2026 → rate exits 2026 at 3.25-3.50%

**Condition B — Persistent inflation (tariff effects durable, PCE stays >3%):**
No cuts in 2026. Dot plot distribution shows 7 participants already in this camp. Rate holds 3.5-3.75% through year-end.

**Condition C — Labor market shock (unemployment rises above 4.5-5%):**
Emergency cut sequence; rate could fall 75-100bps in H2 2026. Currently low probability given April FOMC signal removal of easing bias.

**Policy constraint (operational):** The FFR target sits ABOVE the neutral rate (3.1% longer-run). Current stance = restrictive. Duration of restriction depends on inflation trajectory, not balance sheet.
