## QT Halt — Timeline and Mechanics

**FOMC announcement:** October 29, 2025 — QT halt effective December 1, 2025 [WEB-2026-05-24]

**QT2 final score (June 2022 → November 2025):**
- Total securities reduction: **$2.2 trillion** [WEB-2026-05-24]
  - Treasuries: $1.6T
  - Agency MBS: $600B
- Balance sheet shrank from ~$9T peak (April 2022) → ~$6.7T (May 2026)

**Post-halt reinvestment policy:**
- All Treasury principal payments → reinvested in T-bills
- All agency MBS principal payments → reinvested in T-bills
- Net effect: balance sheet composition shifts toward shorter-duration assets

## Reserve Management Purchases (RMPs)

**Program start:** mid-December 2025 [WEB-2026-05-24]

**Rationale:** QT halt alone insufficient — even without active runoff, other Fed liabilities (TGA, currency) grow over time, naturally draining reserves. RMPs offset this structural drain plus absorb seasonal TGA fluctuations.

**Pace evolution:** [WEB-2026-05-24]
- Dec 2025 – mid-April 2026: **$40B/month** (elevated pace to pre-position ahead of April tax season TGA peak)
- Mid-April → mid-May 2026: **$25B/month** (taper phase 1)
- Mid-May → mid-June 2026: **$10B/month** (taper phase 2)

**Securities purchased:** Exclusively T-bills to date. Authority exists to buy Treasuries ≤3y maturity if needed.

**Announcement schedule:** Monthly pace announced around 9th business day of each month.

**Policy status:** RMPs are NOT QE. They do not change the stance of monetary policy — purpose is purely technical reserve maintenance.

## Balance Sheet Trajectory Forward

```
QT2 end (Dec 1 2025)
    ↓
MBS runoff replaced by T-bills (duration shortening SOMA)
    ↓
RMPs add T-bills to offset organic reserve drain
    ↓
Balance sheet size: STABLE to SLIGHTLY GROWING (RMP pace > organic drain)
    ↓
Once reserves "comfortably ample" confirmed → RMPs taper to zero
    ↓
Long-run equilibrium: SOMA = primarily T-bills + shorter Treasuries
```

## Governor Miran (March 2026) — Further Reduction Potential

Researchers estimate the **remaining reduction potential** within the ample reserves framework = **$1.2–2.1T** from current ~$6.7T. [WEB-2026-05-24]

- Achievable only if: LSM infrastructure added to Fedwire; SRF stigma removed; IORB tiering considered
- Without these enabling steps: reserve floor is effectively ~$3T → further balance sheet reduction risks repo stress

**Key tension (New Fed-Treasury Accord 2026 proposal):** The Warsh-Bessent framework calls for a smaller Fed balance sheet, but the operational reality is that the minimum ample reserve level is structurally high (~$3T). Any attempt to reduce below this without operational reforms risks a Sep 2019-type repo event.
