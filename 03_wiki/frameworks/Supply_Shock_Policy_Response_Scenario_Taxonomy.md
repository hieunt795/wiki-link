## Framework Structure
The taxonomy (formalized by the Bank of England in 2026) uses a three-scenario architecture to parameterize the policy response to an external supply shock (e.g., energy price spike) [RAW-CLIP].

### Scenario A: Transitory Shock
- **Conditions:** Energy prices follow market futures and decline; no significant second-round (behavioral) effects emerge.
- **Inflation Path:** Inflation returns to target within the medium term and may fall below target as direct effects fade.
- **Policy Path:** A less restrictive stance; potential for further easing or maintaining a "hold" before cutting [RAW-CLIP].

### Scenario B: Moderate Persistence
- **Conditions:** Energy prices remain higher for longer with moderate second-round effects in wage- and price-setting.
- **Inflation Path:** Inflation returns to target gradually over a 2-3 year horizon.
- **Policy Path:** An "active hold" or gradual tightening to ensure inflation convergence [RAW-CLIP].

### Scenario C: Adverse Persistence (Non-Linear)
- **Conditions:** Severe and persistent energy shortages (e.g., oil >$130/bbl) combined with strong second-round effects and unanchored expectations.
- **Inflation Path:** Inflation rises significantly above target (e.g., >6%) and remains elevated through the projection horizon.
- **Policy Path:** Forceful and rapid tightening of monetary policy [RAW-CLIP].

## Decision Rules
The framework employs a hierarchy of "look-through" rules based on the type of effect [RAW-CLIP]:
1. **Direct Effects:** Generally looked through as they fade before policy can take effect.
2. **Indirect Effects:** Weaker case for looking through as they take longer to permeate the production network.
3. **Second-Round Effects:** Should **not** be looked through; these represent changes in behavior that require a forceful policy response [RAW-CLIP].

## Advantages of the Framework
- **Optionality:** Preserves policy flexibility on both sides of the risk balance.
- **Transparency:** Substitutes vague forward guidance with explicit state-contingency.
- **Market Alignment:** Allows markets to price in the different scenarios, delivering some of the necessary tightening or easing before the central bank acts [RAW-CLIP].
