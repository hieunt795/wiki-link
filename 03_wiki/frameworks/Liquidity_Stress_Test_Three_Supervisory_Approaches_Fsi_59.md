  use for liquidity stress testing: (1) aggregating bank-run stress tests into a sector-wide
  view (universal baseline); (2) bespoke sector-wide exercises designed by supervisors
  — exemplified by BCB (since early 2000s), ECB/SSM (100 significant institutions,
  2019), and Riksbank; and (3) exploratory bank-NBFI interaction exercises (BoE, MAS)
  — most complex, typically one-off. The third approach remains largely qualitative
  due to data limitations on interbank and bank-NBFI exposures.'
