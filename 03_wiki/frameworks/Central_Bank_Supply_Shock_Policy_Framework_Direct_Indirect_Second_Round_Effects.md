thesis: '[LLM] The standard CB framework for a negative supply shock partitions effects
  into: (1) direct effects — immediate pass-through to headline prices; (2) indirect
  effects — second-order cost increases spreading across the production network; and
  (3) second-round effects — changes in wage- and price-setting behavior that outlast
  the original shock. Policy responds to (3) only, holding through (1) and (2) unless
  longer-term expectations move. This taxonomy is incomplete when the structural production-network
  cascade is present, because the cascade amplifies prices through channels that do
  not require expectations to move first.'
