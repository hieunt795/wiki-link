  and quantify impediments to intragroup liquidity transfer — including regulatory
  ring-fencing (local regulatory requirements preventing outflows), operational constraints
  (systems, FX conversion), and legal restrictions (local insolvency law). The analysis
  must be currency-specific (material currencies identified and monitored separately)
  and assess how much group-level liquidity actually reaches the stressed entity.
  A misleadingly adequate group LCR may mask local deficiencies that the ILAAP must
