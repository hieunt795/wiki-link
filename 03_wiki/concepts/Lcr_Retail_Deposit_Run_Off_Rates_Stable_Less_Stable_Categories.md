  30-day outflow assumptions: (1) Stable deposits — 5% standard run-off rate; 3% if
  the jurisdiction has an effective deposit insurance scheme (prompt payouts, legally
  binding coverage, well-funded, high public awareness). Deposit insurance alone does

  free to add higher buckets for specific risk characteristics (online-only deposits,
