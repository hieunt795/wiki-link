thesis: '[LLM] Reverse stress testing (RST) inverts the stress test logic: instead
  of applying a scenario and measuring impact, it starts from a defined failure outcome
  (e.g. business model failure, liquidity exhaustion, capital breach) and works backward
  to identify scenarios that would cause it. RST is required under EBA GL/2018/04
  for both regular stress testing and recovery planning purposes; it exposes tail
  risks and hidden vulnerabilities in business model assumptions that forward-looking
  scenario analysis may miss.'
