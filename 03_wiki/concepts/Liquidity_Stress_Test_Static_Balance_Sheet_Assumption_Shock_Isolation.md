thesis: '[LLM] Many supervisor-run sector-wide liquidity stress tests (notably ECB/SSM)
  use a static balance sheet assumption: no new inflows after the reference date,
  no contract renegotiations, no management responses modeled. This isolates the pure
  initial shock impact before mitigating actions, enabling clean cross-bank comparison
  and providing an upper bound for inherent vulnerability. Trade-off: it is unrealistic
  (management always reacts) and may overstate survival difficulty while missing dynamic
  amplification effects from collective management responses. Alternative: dynamic
  exercises allowing management responses (BCB, Riksbank approaches) better reflect
  realized stress but are harder to compare across banks.'
