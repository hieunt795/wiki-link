thesis: '[LLM] The normative perspective in the ILAAP is a multi-year forward-looking
  assessment of the bank''s ability to comply with all liquidity regulatory and supervisory
  requirements (LCR, NSFR, Individual Liquidity Guidance) under both baseline and
  adverse scenarios. Unlike the economic perspective (internal risk view), the normative
  perspective is structured around externally imposed ratios. Critical: the normative
  perspective does NOT require LCR ≥ 100% under severe stress — it requires a credible
  plan to remain viable and return to compliance, with the LCR path monitored and
  linked to the risk appetite statement, LCP, and recovery plan.'
