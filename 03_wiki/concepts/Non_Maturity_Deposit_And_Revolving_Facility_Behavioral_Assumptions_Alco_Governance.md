thesis: '[LLM] Non-maturity deposits (savings, current accounts) and revolving facilities
  (overdrafts, credit cards) require behavioral assumptions to replace missing contractual
  maturities in ALM models. ALCO governs these assumptions through an annual review
  cycle: behavioral assumptions (average life, core/non-core deposit split, utilization
  rates) must be back-tested against realized data, stress-tested against historical
  scenarios (rate shocks, credit events), and validated by an independent model validation
  function. Basel IRRBB supervisory expectations (BCBS d368) require banks to document
  and justify behavioral assumptions and update them when conditions change materially.'
