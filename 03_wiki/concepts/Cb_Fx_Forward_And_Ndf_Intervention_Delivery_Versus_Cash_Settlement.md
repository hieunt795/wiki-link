thesis: '[LLM] Outright forwards commit the CB to deliver actual FX at a future date
  at a pre-agreed rate, creating an off-balance-sheet obligation that only affects
  NFA at settlement — allowing the CB to signal rate commitment without immediate
  reserve cost, but creating delivery risk if reserves are insufficient at maturity.
  NDFs (Non-Deliverable Forwards) settle the profit/loss in domestic currency with
  no FX delivery, enabling CB intervention in offshore markets or for restricted currencies
  without touching gross reserves; the tradeoff is that NDF exposure does not appear
  in reserve reports, creating opacity around the true cost of intervention.'
