thesis: '[LLM] A bank liquidity stress test comprises three building blocks: (1) Asset
  treatment — applying haircuts to liquid asset values reflecting market illiquidity
  (fire sale discounts) under stress; (2) Liability treatment — modeling deposit outflow
  rates by counterparty type (retail stable/unstable, corporate, financial institution)
  and run-off of wholesale funding and contingent commitments; (3) Management response
  — actions available (draw credit lines, asset sales, collateral posting with central
  bank) and their impact on the survival horizon. Second-round effects from collective
  management responses across the banking system are the hardest to model and remain
  largely qualitative.'
