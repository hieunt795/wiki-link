## Definition
[LLM] Financial repression: policies that direct private savings into government financing at below-market rates, implicitly taxing savers. Classical form used capital controls; modern form uses reserve creation and YCC.

## Japan Mechanism (2013-2024)
[LLM] BOJ kept IOER at or near 0% on 91% of GDP in bank reserves:
- Government funding cost: ~100bps below nominal GDP growth
- Estimated implicit spread captured: **197bps below market-warranted rates**
- Aggregate cost to depositors: ~2% of GDP per year
- Duration: ~decade (2013 to BoJ rate normalization 2024)

## Instrument Comparison
| Era | Instrument | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-2001 | FILP / postal deposits | Administrative direction of savings |
| 2010- | QE bank reserves | IOER below market, captive banking system |

## Conditions for Success
[LLM] Requires: (1) captive domestic investor base with few alternatives; (2) CB control over short-rate (floor system); (3) household balance sheets concentrated in deposits vs. equities. Japan satisfied all three.
