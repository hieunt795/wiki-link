## FILP Structure (pre-2001)
[LLM] Japanese households deposited savings at Japan Post (largest deposit-taker globally at peak). Postal savings legally required to flow to Ministry of Finance Trust Fund Bureau, channelled via FILP into government-directed lending. FILP portfolio exceeded 100% of GDP in 2001.

## 2001 Reform Break
[LLM] Postal savings and public pension funds freed from mandatory Trust Fund Bureau deposits. FILP forced to raise funds via market-priced FILP bonds. Result: FILP deposits on consolidated government balance sheet fell from 46.4% GDP (1997) to 1.6% GDP (2023).

## QE as Market-Friendly Successor
[LLM] BOJ QE from 2010: banks hold reserves at BoJ at near-zero IOER. Different instrument, identical outcome: domestic financial system funds sovereign at below-market rates. This is market-friendly financial repression replacing administrative repression.

## Implication
[LLM] Each era ends when its instrument fails: FILP ended with postal reform; QE-era repression ending with BoJ rate normalization (2024-).
