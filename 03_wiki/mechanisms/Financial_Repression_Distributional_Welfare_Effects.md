## Duration Ordering (key concept)
[LLM] Effective duration by asset class in Japan:
- Bank deposits: ~0 years
- 10-year JGB: ~7-8 years
- Equities: ~75 years (perpetually growing cash flows discounted at low rate)

## Distributional Mechanics
[LLM] When real rates fall 100bps:
- Non-participant household (deposits only): 0 capital gain, but PV of future consumption rises => welfare LOSS 6-9% of wealth
- Participant household (equity holder): large capital gain (75yr duration effect) => welfare GAIN 17-25% of wealth

## Japan Data (2019 National Survey, ~90,000 households)
[LLM] 67% of Japanese households: no securities beyond deposits (near-zero duration portfolio). Only 16% directly hold equities (28% among top income quartile). Market participation rises with age and income, making repression doubly regressive.

## Policy Incentive Structure
[LLM] Government consolidated balance sheet has NEGATIVE net duration (short liabilities, long assets). This creates structural incentive to suppress real rates indefinitely: lower rates expand fiscal space mechanically. Aging demographics reinforce this incentive via pension obligations.
