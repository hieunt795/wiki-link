thesis: '[LLM] Supply shocks propagate through two analytically distinct amplifiers:
  (1) a structural amplifier — automatic, rooted in input-output architecture of multi-stage
  production networks, triggered once buffer capacity is exhausted and independent
  of expectations; (2) a behavioural amplifier — conditional on labour market tightness
  and salience of inflation to wage- and price-setters (classical second-round effects).
  Standard central bank frameworks subsume the structural channel under indirect effects
  rather than treating it as a separate non-linear propagation mechanism with its
  own policy timing implications.'
