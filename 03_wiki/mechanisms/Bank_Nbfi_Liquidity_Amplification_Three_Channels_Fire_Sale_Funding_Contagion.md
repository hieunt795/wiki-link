thesis: '[LLM] Banks and NBFIs interact in liquidity stress through three amplification
  channels: (1) Asset liquidation — NBFI fire sales (triggered by redemption shocks
  or margin calls) depress prices of assets also held by banks, impairing bank collateral
  values; (2) Funding withdrawal — NBFIs withdraw repo, FX swap, and money market
  funding from banks; (3) Contagion — depositor/investor runs triggered by perceived
  bank-NBFI similarity. March 2020 (dash-for-cash), 2022 UK LDI pension crisis, and
  2023 SVB/Credit Suisse stress all involved at least two of these three channels.
  Authorities have begun designing exploratory bank-NBFI stress tests (BoE, MAS) to
  map these linkages.'
