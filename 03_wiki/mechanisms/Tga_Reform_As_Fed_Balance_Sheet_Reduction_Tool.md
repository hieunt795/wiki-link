## Scope Boundary

[LLM] This node is canonical for TGA reform as a policy lever to reduce the Fed balance-sheet footprint.

[LLM] It should rely on [[Treasury_General_Account_Tga_Reserve_Swap]] for the liability accounting and [[Tga_Volatility_And_Reserve_Buffer_Demand]] for the reserve-buffer channel.

[LLM] It should not duplicate the buffer-sizing formula in [[Ample_Reserves_Buffer_Sizing_Tga_Volatility]].

## Mechanism Role

[LLM] The reform question is distinct from the accounting question: it asks whether Treasury cash-management choices can lower the reserve buffer the Fed must maintain.

[LLM] Lower average TGA levels reduce the steady liability space occupied by Treasury deposits, while lower TGA volatility reduces the precautionary reserve buffer needed for rate control.
