thesis: '[LLM] ILAAP management actions (pre-recovery responses to liquidity stress)
  and recovery plan options (post-trigger crisis measures) are temporally distinct
  but must not overlap: if the bank uses an action under ILAAP (e.g. issues debt to
  boost liquidity buffer), that same action cannot be counted again in the recovery
  plan as available capacity. ECB Principle 2 / Chunk 12 SSM Guide: any material ILAAP
  management action must immediately trigger re-assessment of recovery plan feasibility
  to prevent double-counting of crisis capacity. The bank''s total crisis response
  capacity = ILAAP actions + ADDITIONAL recovery options (not ILAAP actions + ALL
  recovery options).'
