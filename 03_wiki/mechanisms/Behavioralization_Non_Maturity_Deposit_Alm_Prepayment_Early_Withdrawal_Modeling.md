thesis: '[LLM] Behavioralization is the ALM process of converting contractual cash
  flows of products with embedded optionality (non-maturity deposits, overdrafts,
  fixed-rate mortgages with prepayment) into modeled behavioral cash flows for interest
  rate risk and liquidity risk measurement. For term deposits, two behavioral probabilities
  drive the model: renewal probability (fraction that rolls over at maturity) and
  early withdrawal probability (fraction that exits before contractual maturity).
  These probabilities transform the contractual term structure into a behavioral term
  structure used for gap analysis and FTP curve application.'
