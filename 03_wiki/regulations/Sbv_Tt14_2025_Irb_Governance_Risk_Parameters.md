## Governance Requirements (Điều 66)

**Board responsibilities:**
- Understand and approve IRB system design (incl. risk parameters, stress testing)
- Oversee CEO implementation of IRB; review significant issues annually

**CEO/management responsibilities:**
- Maintain suitability of IRB system
- Publish at least annual reports to Board on: rating profiles, rating migrations, PD/LGD/EAD actual-vs-estimated comparisons, stress test results, validation results

**Credit Risk Control Unit (Bộ phận kiểm soát rủi ro tín dụng):**
- Organizationally independent from credit origination
- Functions: rating review, stress testing, validation, model performance monitoring, model lifecycle governance
- Internal/external audit review at least annually

## Use Test (Điều 67)

The IRB system must be used in credit approval, risk management, internal capital allocation, and reporting — not just for capital calculation.

Banks must demonstrate at least **2 consecutive years** of IRB use (meeting minimum requirements) before filing for SBV approval to apply IRB. If IRB is revised toward lower conservatism, the 2-year clock resets.

## Risk Parameter Estimation (Điều 68)

**PD (Probability of Default):**
- Must equal the long-run average 1-year default rate for each rating grade
- Permitted methods: internal historical data, external data, pooled data sources (combined use)
- Must apply margin of conservatism (biên độ thận trọng) proportional to uncertainty in data/methods
- PD estimates must be reviewed at least annually

**LGD (Loss Given Default):**
- Defined as economic loss (includes all direct and indirect recovery costs)
- Must reflect realized losses, not contractual amounts

**EAD (Exposure at Default):**
- Must be estimated for off-balance-sheet items

**General standards:**
- Cannot rely solely on expert judgment; must be data-driven
- Data sample must be representative of the bank's actual portfolio and credit standards
- Out-of-sample tests required to validate accuracy and robustness
