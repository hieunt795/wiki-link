thesis: '[LLM] The ECB ILAAP Guide (Nov 2018) formalises 7 supervisory principles
  for significant institutions under the SSM: (1) management body accountable for
  ILAAP governance; (2) ILAAP integral to overall management framework; (3) ILAAP
  ensures liquidity adequacy from economic AND normative perspectives — both must
  complement each other; (4) all material risks identified; (5) internal liquidity
  buffers are high-quality and clearly defined; (6) risk quantification methodologies
  are adequate, consistent, independently validated; (7) regular stress testing ensures
  adequacy in adverse conditions. The ECB assesses ILAAPs against these 7 principles
  within SREP.'
