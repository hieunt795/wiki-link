thesis: '[LLM] EBA Guidelines GL/2016/10 (Nov 2016, apply from Jan 2017) specify what
  ICAAP and ILAAP information competent authorities must collect from institutions
  for SREP assessment. Four information categories: (1) general — governance, risk
  management, stress testing frameworks, business model; (2) ICAAP-specific — methodology
  design, internal capital estimates, capital planning; (3) ILAAP-specific — funding
  strategy, liquidity buffers, collateral, intraday liquidity, CFP; and (4) conclusions
  and quality assurance. The guidelines do not prescribe specific ICAAP/ILAAP formats
  but standardise supervisory information requirements.'
