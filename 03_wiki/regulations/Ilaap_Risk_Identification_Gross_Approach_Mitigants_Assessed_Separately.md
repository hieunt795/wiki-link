thesis: '[LLM] ECB ILAAP Principle 4 mandates a two-step risk identification process:
  (1) ''Gross approach'' — identify all material risks before any mitigation (hedging,
  netting, collateral, insurance); (2) separately assess mitigation effectiveness
  under stress. This prevents the common failure of hiding material risks behind assumed
  mitigation that may not function under stress (e.g. hedges that fail in correlated
  market crises, collateral haircuts that increase when needed most). Risk cannot
  be excluded because it is difficult to quantify — conservative estimates are required.'
