## Three Lines of Defense (Điều 4)

**Line 1 — Risk-originating units (tuyến bảo vệ thứ nhất):**
Revenue-generating functions and units making risk decisions; responsible for first-level identification, control, and risk mitigation within their activities.

**Line 2 — Oversight functions (tuyến bảo vệ thứ hai):**
Minimum: Compliance unit + Risk Management unit. Functions:
- Develop risk management policies and internal regulations
- Measure, monitor, and control bank-wide risk and regulatory compliance

**Line 3 — Internal Audit (tuyến bảo vệ thứ ba):**
Performed by the Internal Audit unit under the Board Supervisory Committee. Independent of Line 1 and 2.

**Model risk exception:** Model risk management applies its own 3-line structure per Article 56.

## Governance Committees (Điều 6)

At **CEO level** (tham mưu Tổng giám đốc):
- **Risk Council (Hội đồng rủi ro):** Chaired by the dedicated CRO (not CEO); meets at minimum quarterly. Advises on risk management decisions and risk appetite.
- **ALCO (Hội đồng ALCO):** Chaired by CEO or another senior executive; meets at minimum quarterly. Advises on Asset/Liability Management.
- **Capital Management Council (Hội đồng quản lý vốn):** Chaired by CEO or CFO; meets at minimum semi-annually. Oversees ICAAP process.

At **Board level** (Điều 6(2)):
- Risk Management Committee (Ủy ban quản lý rủi ro): majority non-executive members
- Personnel Committee (Ủy ban nhân sự): majority non-executive members

## Material Risk Types (Điều 3)

Required to manage: credit risk (customer + counterparty), market risk (interest rate, FX, equity price, commodity), operational risk (including legal risk), liquidity risk, concentration risk, IRRBB (interest rate risk in banking book), model risk, reputational risk, strategic risk.

IRRBB sub-components: Gap risk, Basis risk, Option risk (automatic + behavioural).
